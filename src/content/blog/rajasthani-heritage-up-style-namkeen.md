Namkeen-making is a beautiful, time-honored art. If you trace its roots, this savory tradition belongs to the royal kitchens and arid lands of Rajasthan. That is where the mastery of crisp textures and balanced spices truly began. But here in Muzaffarnagar, we wanted to bring that heritage closer to our roots, blending the patient craftsmanship of Rajasthan with the robust, comforting preparation style of Uttar Pradesh.

At Hariram, we do things differently than the large industrial brands. From their very first day, those mass-market factories chose refined vegetable or palm oil to keep costs low and shelf lives long. We made a deliberate choice to take a different path. We use only Kachi Ghani (cold-pressed, unrefined) mustard oil. It is more expensive and has a shorter shelf life, but it gives our namkeen its signature, honest flavor. For us, this business is personal. We live by the philosophy of "Grahak Dev Tulya"—treating our customers with the same respect, honesty, and purity we would offer to God.

## Ground Fresh Daily in Our Muzaffarnagar Kitchen

We do not buy pre-mixed industrial masalas. Instead, we grind our spices fresh in-house every single day to ensure the natural oils and aromas remain intact. Every packet of our namkeen is made in small batches in our home kitchen, entirely free from the rushed, mechanical feel of bulk factory production.

We want you to feel good about what you share with your family. That is why our home-kitchen operations are fully FSSAI licensed (Lic. 22725393001461). We do not claim to make the "world's best" snacks, but we do promise absolute honesty in our ingredients. From our home in Muzaffarnagar to yours, this is our way of keeping a beautiful tradition alive, made fresh just for you.
