The honest truth? Most of these sweets sit untouched, lose their freshness, or get casually re-gifted to neighbors.

During Indian festivals, almost every relative, colleague, and client brings sweets. By day two, guests actively crave something **crisp, savory, spiced, and light**.

When someone opens a premium box of artisanal namkeen, it does not get relegated to the back of the pantry:

- **Instant Gratification**: It is opened immediately when evening tea is brewed.

- **Daily Utility**: While sweets are eaten in guilt-ridden bites, savoury snacks are enjoyed by the entire household for weeks.
