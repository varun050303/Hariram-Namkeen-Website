Last week, we received a phone call from a customer in Noida that made our entire kitchen team smile. He had just tried our Sev for the first time.

"Usually, when I eat namkeen," he told us, "I have to wash my hands with soap immediately. My fingers get coated in this sticky, white layer of grease, and my throat feels heavy for hours. With your namkeen, my hands were clean, and my stomach felt light."

## The Secret of the Clean Finger Test

What this gentleman noticed is the honest difference between industrial fat and real oil. From day one, large commercial brands have used palm oil or refined vegetable oils. They did not switch to these oils recently; they chose them from the very start because they are incredibly cheap and keep packets shelf-stable for many months. But palm oil has a high melting point, which is why it leaves that stubborn, waxy film on your palate and fingers.

At Hariram, we made a different choice. In our small home-kitchen in Muzaffarnagar, we cook exclusively in pure Kachi Ghani (cold-pressed, unrefined) mustard oil. It is significantly more expensive, and because it is minimally processed, it has a shorter shelf life. But it is clean, wholesome, and digests naturally without leaving you feeling heavy.

We believe in the old-world philosophy of "Grahak Dev Tulya"—treating the customer with the same respect and purity we would offer to God. We do not use industrial shortcuts. Our spices are not bought as pre-mixed packets from commercial suppliers; we grind them fresh in-house every single morning to preserve their natural oils and aroma.
