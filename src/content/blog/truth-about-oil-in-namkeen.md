When you open a packet of namkeen, what does it smell like? In our Muzaffarnagar home-kitchen, it smells of freshly ground cloves, black pepper, and the sharp, warm aroma of pure Kachi Ghani mustard oil.

For decades, the big industrial brands have made a different choice. From their very first day, they opted for refined palm oil or sunflower oil. It makes sense for them—these oils are cheap, neutral in taste, and keep packets sitting on supermarket shelves for months. But at Hariram Namkeen, we chose a different path, even though cold-pressed mustard oil costs more and gives our namkeen a shorter, natural shelf life.

## The Honest Difference: Kachi Ghani vs. Refined Oils

For health-conscious families, the type of fat we consume daily matters. Refined palm oil is highly processed and loaded with saturated fats. On the other hand, unrefined, cold-pressed Kachi Ghani mustard oil is naturally rich in monounsaturated fatty acids and contains a balanced ratio of Omega-3 and Omega-6 fatty acids. Unlike refined sunflower oil, which is heavily processed using heat and chemicals, our mustard oil is extracted gently without high heat, preserving its natural nutrients and distinct character.

We believe in transparency. We don't claim our namkeen is a health supplement, but we do promise that we use ingredients that respect your body.

The art of namkeen-making traces its roots back to the dry sands of Rajasthan. Here in Western UP, we have blended that rich Rajasthani heritage with our local, robust UP-style preparation. Every batch is made in small quantities, using spices ground fresh in our kitchen daily—never pre-mixed industrial masalas.
