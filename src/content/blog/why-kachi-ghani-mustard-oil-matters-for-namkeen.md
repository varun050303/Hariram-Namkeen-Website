## The Truth About Kachi Ghani vs. Refined Oil

In simple terms, Kachi Ghani is the traditional method of extracting oil. Mustard seeds are crushed slowly at room temperature using a heavy wooden press. Because there is no high heat or harsh chemical solvents involved, the oil retains its natural nutrients, rich golden color, and that signature pungent aroma. It is completely unrefined and pure.

In contrast, refined oils—like palm oil or refined vegetable oils—go through extreme heat, bleaching, and chemical processing to strip away their natural smell and color. This makes them cheap and extends their shelf life, which is why large industrial brands have used them from day one. They didn't "switch" to these oils; they chose them from the start to keep costs low.

At Hariram, we made a deliberate choice to do things differently. Real Kachi Ghani mustard oil costs more and has a shorter shelf life, but it is far better for your health and gives our namkeen an authentic, rustic taste that refined oils simply cannot match.
