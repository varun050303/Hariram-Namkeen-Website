When you open a packet of our mustard oil namkeen, you are met with a distinctive, warm aroma. We fry every batch in pure yellow mustard oil (peeli sarson ka tel) in our family-run kitchen in Muzaffarnagar, Uttar Pradesh. We chose yellow mustard oil because of a simple belief: oil is something the body needs, so rather than avoiding it, we chose to use an oil we trust completely.

## The Aroma and the Sharpness

Yellow mustard oil behaves uniquely in the kadhai. It carries a gentle sharpness and a clean, recognizable fragrance that reaches you the moment you break the seal. When our halwais heat the oil, our entire kitchen fills with that familiar aroma.

We do not try to mask that character. That natural pungency works quietly alongside the gram flour, lentils, and spices. Customers often tell us that the taste is good, that the aroma when the packet opens is distinctive, and that they are able to eat namkeen without worrying about health concerns. For us, that comes down to honesty about what goes into the kadhai. We never use palm oil, and we use no preservatives. What you taste is simply pure yellow mustard oil, spices, and dough.

## Prepared to Order in Small Batches

We do not make namkeen in bulk stock to sit on warehouse shelves. In our Muzaffarnagar kitchen, our halwais work in small batches of 15-20 kg at a time. Every single batch is prepared to order.

Before any batch is sealed, our halwais crunch-test and taste it. Only when the texture is crisp and the flavor is right does it move to packing. Everything is packed right here in Muzaffarnagar and shipped directly across India.
