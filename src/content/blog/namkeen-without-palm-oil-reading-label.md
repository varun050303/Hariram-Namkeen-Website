To find namkeen without palm oil, turn the packet over and read the ingredient list directly under the oil declaration. The oil line shows exactly what the snack was fried in, and it should name the specific oil plainly. If you want to know what you are eating, reading that single line gives you the clearest answer.

In our family kitchen in Muzaffarnagar, Uttar Pradesh, we believe in complete honesty about what goes into the kadhai. For us, oil is not something to hide behind vague wording. Oil is something the body needs, so instead of avoiding it, we chose a good one: pure yellow mustard oil (peeli sarson ka tel). We never use palm oil in any of our preparations.

## What to Look for in the Oil Line

When you inspect a packet of namkeen, look past the front cover and check the ingredients panel on the back. 

1. **A specific, single oil name:** Check whether the oil used is clearly identified. We state yellow mustard oil openly because that is the only oil we pour into our kadhai.
2. **Absence of additives:** Check whether the packet lists preservatives. Good namkeen does not need them. In our kitchen, we use no preservatives at all.
3. **Freshness indicators:** Freshly made namkeen carries its own natural freshness. We prepare our namkeen to order rather than keeping bulk stock waiting on shelves.
